# Motion Genesis V6 — Walking with recorded human motion

V5 looked mechanical because a procedural sine-wave gait and strong IK overrode the pose of the hips and legs. V6 introduces genuinely recorded human movement, maintaining the educational 206-bone NASA Z-2 suit.

## Measured provenance

- Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 02, clip 02_01, described as walk.
- Source: https://github.com/Shriinivas/cmubvh/blob/main/Sequence-001-009/02/Data/02_01.zip
- University: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
- Original BVH SHA256: cf56db43157acc3d200b3d4215523f54d354a8926bea67e1f6c8031bd335ba7e
- Original motion: 344 frames sampled at 120 Hz.
- Retargeted cycle: 130 original frames (~1.083 s); 66 samples; 20 animated anatomical bone regions.
- The original source ZIP remains in mocap/cmu/02_01_walk_original.zip, checked into GitHub for reproducibility.
- The lightweight browser JSON is generated by scripts/retarget_cmu_walk.py, which uses SciPy only at build time.

## Improvements

- Human thigh/knee flexion, ankle and toe rotations, pelvis sway, counter-rotating arms, torso and head motion.
- Walking phase synchronized with actual distance travelled; smoothly blended into idle breathing.
- Partial planted-foot IK correction on measured terrain only. The recorded gait remains dominant.
- Slope correction does not fabricate missing elevations. Individual rocks smaller than CTX cells remain unknown.
- No 206 full-body physics simulations are executed. Only externally relevant suit surfaces are weighted.
- Foot-movement tests run in actual Chrome WebGL and record sequential real screenshots.

## Limitations

The CMU actor was a human walking without a spacesuit. This animation is retargeted to NASA's conceptual Z-2 external shell and is NOT certified NASA Martian-EVA biomechanics or motion capture of an astronaut in a suit. The external NASA suit model does not contain independent tiny medical bones or individual finger geometry. The recorded motion is real, but the rig approximation and height-adjusted contact are educational.
